# Diaspora Co. (https://www.diasporaco.com/) - DEEP ANALYSIS

US single-origin Indian spice brand (founder Sana Javeri Kadri). The benchmark for **mission-led spice storytelling**.
Screens: `screens/diaspora-*.png`. Raw: `raw/www.diasporaco.com.html`.

## Tech
- **Shopify**, theme **"Pebble"** (schema_name; a Shopify Horizon-family theme: `motion-component.js`, `motion-coordinator.js`, `page-transition.js`, `morph.js`, `section-renderer.js`, `animation.js`). Swiper (carousels, fade effect, 3D), **Lenis** referenced in code (smooth scroll), PhotoSwipe, Klaviyo, Stamped reviews, Gorgias chat, GoAffPro, Smile-style loyalty widget, cart reward progress bar ("last-minute essentials" upsell).
- No Three.js/WebGL. Motion is page transitions + scroll reveals + sliders.

## Typography
- Display/headings: **P22 Mackinac Pro** (Typekit; warm quirky serif - bold, slightly irregular), used in magenta for nav + titles + product names.
- Body: **Fustat** (humanist sans) + Source Sans.
- Free equivalents: **Fraunces** or **DM Serif Display** (heading), **DM Sans/Figtree** (body).

## Palette
Hot **magenta `#D5137B`** (nav bar, buttons, headlines, quote block), **sunshine yellow `#FFCF01`/`#FFD000`** (USP strip, CTA on popups), deep forest green (~`#33663B`) + pale sage (~`#E2E6CB`) (sourcing block), cream `#FFFDF0`/`#FFFCEB`, navy `#080A5C` accents, orange `#F97316` announcement bar, text `#444`. Bold, unapologetically South-Asian-saturated, un-"gourmet-brown".

## Homepage structure
1. Orange announcement bar (seasonal drop).
2. **Floating magenta rounded nav pill** over hero (logo wordmark, Shop All, Spices, Bundle & Save, Recipes, Gifts, Discover, search field).
3. **Full-bleed lifestyle hero** (holiday table: chai latte, cookies, spice tins), left-aligned white serif headline "Our Big Holiday Drop Is Here!", short line, magenta CTA.
4. **Yellow USP strip** with hand-illustrated icons: Single Origin / Fair Wages / Freshest Harvest / Highest Quality (diamond separators).
5. **Most Popular** product carousel (Swiper): cream cards with magenta serif names, price, short description, full-width magenta ADD TO CART, tiny SAVE badges.
6. **Press/mission split**: left photo of women farmers with baskets; right magenta panel with giant serif press quote.
7. **Recipes Our Community Loves**: recipe cards (3-Step Spiced Chai, Dishoom's Jackfruit Biryani, Alison Roman's turmeric tea cake, Ghee Coffee...) - community/chef names as credibility.
8. **"Sourced from 150+ farms across India & Sri Lanka"**: forest-green panel with illustrated woman picking flowers + horizontal story cards (farm photos, farmer portrait, short paragraphs).
9. **What Sets Us Apart** (comparison/benefits), **FAQ accordion**, footer, loyalty widget, 10%-off tab + email popup (magenta, yellow CTA, illustrated hands holding sprout).

## Product card
Cream card, thin rounded border, image top (tin photos on coloured background), magenta serif title, price (+ strikethrough + green "SAVE $2" chip), 2-line benefit copy, **full-width magenta ADD TO CART bar attached to the card bottom**. "Low stock!" red micro-label.

## Copy tone
Mission-forward, first-person-plural, warm and political ("radically reimagine a spice trade that's equitable, climate-resilient, and undeniably delicious"), named farmers & farms, recipe-first (spices as ingredients in real dishes), cookbook tie-in.

## What makes it premium
Radical transparency (farm names, wages, harvest dates), illustration language that feels *by* the diaspora, saturated colour confidence, strong human photography, recipes as content. Not minimalist: colourful, joyful.

## Lessons to steal
Origin-story block with farm cards, "single origin / fair wages / freshest harvest" icons, recipe tie-ins, magenta-yellow-green unexpected palette, loyalty/threshold rewards.
