# Burlap & Barrel (https://www.burlapandbarrel.com/) - DEEP ANALYSIS

US single-origin spice company, direct-trade with small farms. Hero tag: "Small farms, big flavor."
Screens: `screens/burlap-*.png` (a Klaviyo email popup covers part of each capture - itself a finding: aggressive lead-capture). Raw: `raw/www.burlapandbarrel.com.html`.

## Tech
- **Shopify, theme "Enterprise" (Envision)**. **Flickity 2.3** + flickity-fade (hero / product / press-quote sliders), `animate-on-scroll.js/.css` (simple reveal), instant.page, Klaviyo, Stamped reviews, Gorgias, Convert.com A/B testing, GTM. No GSAP/Three.
- Heavy conversion tooling: free-gift threshold ("Spend $70, get free woven kitchen towels"), referral, subscription Spice Club, quick-add, predictive search.

## Typography
- Display: **Monthoers** (custom condensed slab/woodtype, uppercase, maroon) - used for "10 YEARS OF THE WORLD'S BEST SPICES", "BEST SELLERS", "SPICE SETS & GIFTS". Also "Recoleta" (demo) seen in CSS.
- Body: **Poppins** 400-700.
- Free approx: **Oswald / Bebas Neue / Alfa Slab One** for display + **Poppins**.

## Palette
Burgundy/maroon **`#7F2629`** (brand, logo, press bar, buttons) with red `#A2252A`, warm paper `#F9F8F4`, pale sky announcement bar (~`#C5DCF0`), confetti accents in orange/blue/mustard, near-black `#121212/#1A1A1A`, text `#3D4246`. Label pattern: red woven-basket weave motif (also on kitchen towels).

## Homepage structure
1. Pale-blue announcement bar (free shipping $55+, refer a friend).
2. Header: logo with weave icon, "All Spices | Shop | Bundle & Save | Recipes | Learn", big outlined search bar, account, cart.
3. **Hero (10th-anniversary)**: confetti-strewn pale backdrop, floating paper card with maroon condensed headline + 13,000+ five-star rating + "SHOP BESTSELLERS"; right half: **overhead flat-lay of jars at angles** (Buffalo Ginger, Za'atar, Silk Chili, Black Urfa) with a bowl of spice.
4. **Press quote bar** (maroon): rotating NYT / America's Test Kitchen / etc. logos + quotes (Flickity fade).
5. Doodle-pattern background (hand-drawn spice icons) behind **Best Sellers** carousel (jars on terracotta spice-pile photography; sets).
6. "As seen on Shark Tank" band; **Spice Sets & Gifts**; **Jane Goodall legacy** partnership (cause); **Wild Woodland Honeys**; **"Beautiful spices, equitably sourced"** (farmer partnership, with origin map-like stories); newsletter ("subscribe to our spicy newsletter"); "Extending our impact"; big footer.

## Product card
Photo of the jar sitting on a heap of the actual ground spice (colour-matched hero textures: terracotta chili, golden cardamom), name, tiny origin line (Sumatra, Turkey...), stars (Stamped), price per size, quick-add.

## Copy tone
Straightforward, confident, slightly nerdy and punny ("spicy newsletter"), origin-specific ("Silk Chili - Kahramanmaras, Turkey"), citations of press.

## Premium cues
Single-origin specificity on every label, jar-on-its-own-spice photography, woven-pattern brand device, press proof, partnerships, tactile packaging (towels). More "friendly artisanal" than luxury.

## Lessons
Origin-on-label, pile-of-spice backdrops, brand pattern as repeating motif, press-bar social proof, mission/impact modules.
